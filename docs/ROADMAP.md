# Solmoria roadmap

## Phase 1 – Exploration Foundation (this branch)
- Seeded procedural terrain, streamed in chunks around the player
- Painterly ground shader, instanced wind-animated grass that bends away from the player
- Trees, bushes, rocks, flower clusters, soft contact shadows
- Player: WASD / arrows, dynamic touch joystick, procedural idle / walk / run animation
- High-angle damped follow camera (~55 degrees) matching the reference video
- Directional light + shadows, bloom, vignette, mist, god rays, cloud shadows, pollen
- Low / medium / high quality tiers

## Next
- **Phase 1.5** – replace the procedural character with a GLB; real device profiling; optional foliage LOD
- **Phase 2 – Life**: ambient beetles and fauna with wander AI, day/night, audio
- **Phase 3 – Interaction**: combat prototype (slash VFX, hit reactions), physics if needed
- **Phase 4 – World**: biomes, landmarks, minimap
- **Phase 5 – RPG layer**: stats, items, quests, saving
