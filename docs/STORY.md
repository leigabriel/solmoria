# Legend of Lhei: story bible

## Premise
Long ago a tree of living light, the **Sunseed**, grew at the heart of Solmoria and warmed its ten realms. The **Hollow Night** split the Sunseed into ten shards and scattered them. Colour is fading from the land.

**Lhei**, a small samurai in a straw kasa carrying an old katana, wakes in Dawnmeadow to the song of the falls. Villagers say the shards still hum where the realms are strongest.

## Structure
Ten chapters, one per realm (biome). The journal tracks which realms Lhei has entered. Shard collection, guardians and the Hollow Gate are planned for the next phase.

## Cast
- **Lhei**: the hero. Quiet, stubborn, curious about everything that glows.
- **The Elder**: knows the old story and names the order to walk the realms.
- **Farmer, Merchant, Child, Guard**: village voices; each hints at the fading colour and the shards.
- **The Hollow Night**: the faceless force behind the break. It is thickest and quietest in Hollowdeep.

## Tone
Warm, small-scale and a little melancholy: a painterly adventure where each realm restores one colour.

## Next story beats
1. Shard guardians per realm (boss encounters using the katana moves).
2. Collecting shards into the bag and re-lighting the Sunseed in Dawnmeadow.
3. The Hollow Gate in Moonbloom Grove and the final choice.
